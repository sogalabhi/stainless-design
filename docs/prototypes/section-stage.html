    Where the numbers come from

    Every value was returned by the stainless-csm engine for the app's Load example section and recorded on 2026-11-10. Curves are the engine's own chart points. Working lines are printed as the engine wrote them.

    What is drawn, not calculated

    The section outline is to scale from the typed dimensions. The stress block joins the engine's points on the B.4 curve; the resistance is the formula result, never an integral of the picture. The yield fibre follows from a straight strain line.

    Prototype shortcuts

    Inputs are fixed. While you drag, the page reads between recorded engine points. In the app, the engine would return each frame, and percentages would be engine values rather than a ratio of two of them.
